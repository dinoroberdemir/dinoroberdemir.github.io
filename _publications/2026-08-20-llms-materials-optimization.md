## Overview

We investigate whether open-weight large language models can select promising
materials to evaluate next in finite-pool optimization. We compare five LLMs
with random selection and Gaussian-process acquisition methods across four
materials search tasks.

LLM policies generally improve on random selection, while comparisons with
Gaussian-process methods vary by task. The results highlight both the potential
of LLM acquisition policies and their sensitivity to the model, initialization,
and presentation of candidates.
